# ZERO ROT parameter evidence audit (registry v1.0.0, reviewed 2026-11-05)

Generated by `backend/scripts/build_registry.py`. Every coefficient used by the decay engine is listed with its value, source, conditions, classification and limitations.

**Calibration caveat.** `k_ref` is calibrated so that quality falls from 100 to the minimum sellable score (40) exactly at the published storage life. This guarantees the curve hits Q_min at day L by construction; it does NOT validate the shape of the deterioration curve. It will be upgraded only if experimental quality-over-time observations are found and fitted.

**Weight loss (W) and rejection (R) functions are engineering assumptions.** The humidity and chilling penalty shapes are engineering assumptions anchored on published range edges.
